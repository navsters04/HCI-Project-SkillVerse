Our History

VisualSkill was founded in 2026 by a group of passionate cardists who recognized a gap in structured cardistry education. What started as small workshop sessions in Pearl quickly grew into a comprehensive online platform.

Over the past three years, we have trained over 5,000 students from 40 countries, published 50+ courses, and built a thriving community of practitioners who support and inspire each other.

Our journey continues as we expand our curriculum, develop new teaching methodologies, and push the boundaries of what cardistry education can achieve.

Our Vision

To become the world leading platform for cardistry education, making the art form accessible to anyone with the passion to learn, regardless of background or experience level.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, structured cardistry education that emphasizes precision, creativity, and personal expression. We believe in learning by doing, with expert guidance every step of the way.
